## Core AI Enhancements and Features:

**1. Smart Prioritization & Scheduling:**
* **Learning Your Habits:** AI could observe when you typically complete certain 
  types of tasks (e.g., workouts in the morning, creative work in the evening) 
  and proactively suggest the best time slots for new tasks.
* **Dynamic Prioritization:** Instead of just "high/medium/low," AI could 
  suggest priorities based on deadlines, your energy levels, and dependencies 
  between tasks.

**2. Intelligent Task Suggestions:**
